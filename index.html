About Style ME Interiors

More Than Interiors.
It’s a Community.

Style ME Interiors Dubai is a trusted resource for connecting you with qualified and experienced home improvement professionals in Dubai. Whether you’re planning a renovation, furnishing a new space or simply looking for the right expert — we make it easier to find the people who bring your vision to life.

Founded by Gabby Garvey, Style ME Interiors Dubai brings the city’s home-improvement talent together in one organised, considered place.
